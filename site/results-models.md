## Models and inference

- **M0** fixed 2 × 2 category labels; **M1** stimulus-specific adaptation; **M2** leaky Bayesian learner of sound-to-sound transitions (local surprise); **M3** leaky Bayesian learner of the block rule (global surprise). Equations are in [](methods.md#learners).
- Two preregistered combinations were also fitted: M4 (M2 + M3) and M5 (M1 + M3).
- Half-lives and the block-reset setting were selected by nested leave-one-block-out cross-validation, so all scores are held-out log-likelihoods.
- Group inference: random-effects Bayesian model selection {cite:p}`Stephan2009` with protected exceedance probabilities and the Bayesian omnibus risk {cite:p}`Rigoux2014`.

## Control: model recovery

- Data were simulated from each model with each participant's fitted parameters and noise, then put through the full pipeline.
- Both learners were recovered reliably in both windows: M2 100% early and 90% late; M3 100% in both. The dissociation cannot reflect either learner being undetectable in one window.
- M4 and M5 were not recoverable (M4: 10% early, 15% late; M5: 60% early, 15% late), so they are shown but not interpreted.
- **Limitation:** in the early window, data generated by M0 were attributed to M2 in 45% of simulations.

## Early window (80–140 ms): the local learner wins

- PXP(M2) = 0.94, BOR = 0.022, expected frequency 0.51. Best model in 10/15 participants (M1: 3, M0: 2).
- M2 minus rival (held-out log-likelihood per participant): vs M3 +18.8 (15/15); vs M1 +4.4 (12/15); vs M0 +2.2 (10/15).
- M2's surprise reproduced the four condition means (*r* = 0.99); M3's did not (*r* = 0.48). Descriptive only (four points).
- MMN amplitude increased across thirds of M2 surprise among local deviants: slope *t*(14) = −5.4, *p* < .001, 15/15 ([](#fig2)F, top).

## Late window (300–700 ms): the global learner wins

- PXP(M3) = 0.95, BOR = 0.035, expected frequency 0.54. Best model in 10/15 participants (M4: 2, M0: 2, M5: 1).
- M3 minus rival: vs M2 +30.2 (15/15); vs M1 +34.4 (15/15); vs M0 +1.5 (11/15).
- M3 reproduced the condition means (*r* = 0.99); M2 did not (*r* = 0.28).
- P3b amplitude increased across thirds of M3 surprise among global deviants: slope *t*(14) = 5.2, *p* < .001, 14/15 ([](#fig2)F, bottom).

## What the data do and do not support

- **Late window: learning beyond the condition labels is supported.** The P3b–surprise relation held within condition, after removing each 2 × 2 cell mean: *t*(14) = 5.0, *p* = .0002, 13/15.
- **Early window: weak support for learning within condition.** The binned within-condition test was not significant (*t*(14) = −1.8, *p* = .087, 10/15); a single-trial regression was (−0.47 µV/bit, *t*(14) = −2.7, *p* = .018). Given the modest M2 advantage over M0 (10/15) and the M0 → M2 confusion in recovery, the early result supports local rather than global surprise. It does not establish learning rather than fixed categories.
- **The dissociation is complete for the P3b and near-complete for the MMN.** Local surprise added nothing to the late window once global surprise was accounted for (−0.01 µV/bit, *t*(14) = 0.2, *p* = .84). Global surprise added a small contribution to the early window (−0.10 µV/bit, *t*(14) = −2.6, *p* = .019), about a fifth of the local effect.
- **Timescales and reset are not identifiable at this SNR.** The learners' fitted half-lives did not differ between levels (Wilcoxon *p* = .055), but this null is uninformative. Candidate local-learner regressors correlate *r* = 0.91–0.998. In simulation at the empirical noise level, cross-validation recovered the generating half-life and reset in 0/10 cases (8/10 at ten times the effect size), although M2 still won ([](validation.md)). Model-family conclusions stand; parameter-level claims are not made.
- **Expectations appear to be rebuilt at block boundaries, with individual variation.** Carry-over from the previous block was estimated at ρ = 0 (95% CI 0–0.1); a direct test favoured no carry-over (BF₀₁ = 3.8); reset gave higher held-out R² in 10/15 (*t*(14) = 2.75, *p* = .016). Full carry-over (ρ = 1) fitted best in 3/15 participants.
- **Model space.** Without M4 and M5, PXP(M2) early fell to 0.90 (BOR 0.085) and PXP(M3) late rose to 0.97. The preregistered six-model results are primary.
